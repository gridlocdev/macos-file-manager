# The sidebar

The sidebar on the left has four parts, from the top:

```
Pinned          your favourite folders, in your order
Recent          folders you visited lately
Locations       your Mac's disk, drives and network shares (with Eject)
Folders         a tree of your home folder
```

Click anything to open it. Right-click for **Open in New Tab** and more.

## Pinned

Like Explorer's Quick access. It starts with your home folder, Desktop, Documents, Downloads,
Applications and iCloud Drive (if you use it). Pins are shared by every window.

- **Pin a folder**: drag it from the list into the Pinned section, **between** two pinned
  folders (a line shows where it goes). Or select it and choose **File → Add to Sidebar**
  (Ctrl+Cmd+T), or right-click → **Add to Sidebar**. With nothing selected, Add to Sidebar pins
  the folder you're in.
- **Reorder**: drag pinned folders up and down.
- **Unpin**: right-click → **Remove from Sidebar**. You can remove the starting ones too.

Dropping files **on** a pinned folder (rather than between two) moves or copies them into it,
just like dropping on a folder in the list. See [Drag and drop](Drag-and-Drop.md).

## Recent

The last eight folders you opened, newest first, leaving out ones that are pinned. Right-click
one to **Add to Sidebar** or **Remove from Recent**.

## Locations

Your Mac's startup disk, plus any USB drives, disk images and network shares that are
connected. The list updates when you connect or eject one.

Drives you can eject have an **⏏** button; you can also right-click → **Eject**. If something
is still using the drive, File Manager says so and leaves it connected.

Each location opens like a folder in the tree below: click its arrow to see the folders inside.

## Folders

A tree of your home folder, like Explorer's navigation pane. It **opens down to the folder
you're in** as you move around, and highlights it. Click the arrows to open and close folders;
subfolders are read when you open them.

Hidden folders show in the tree when hidden files are shown (Cmd+H).
